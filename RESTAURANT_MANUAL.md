# Restaurant partner manual

Sign in with the restaurant owner/manager/staff account assigned to the restaurant.

Dashboard shows live active orders. For a newly placed order, Accept it, then mark Start prep and Ready for pickup as work progresses. Invalid lifecycle jumps are rejected by the API.

Menu operations are ownership-protected. Items support price, availability, stock, variants and add-ons. Mark unavailable/sold-out products before they can reach checkout.

Restaurant pause temporarily stops new ordering. Opening hours and holiday/closure data are stored server-side.

Revenue is recorded in restaurant settlements after delivery. Support tickets and reviews are available through platform workflows. Never share customer details outside what is necessary to fulfil the active order.
